Why simple?
- Makes you move faster
- Reading and understanding your system should not be difficult.

Problem solving lessons:
- The problem you are solving is not the the real problem, you still need to collect more variables.
- If the solution is complex, you are surely patch working.

Programming paradigms that keep things simple
- Composition
- Single source of truth
- One direction data flow
- Single responsibiilty principle
- Decoupling
