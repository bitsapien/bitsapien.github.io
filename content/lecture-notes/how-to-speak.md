* How to start ?
  * Don't start with a joke, start with a promise of empowerment.
* 4 samples
  * Cycle around the subject to make it register in their heads.
  * Build a fence around the subject, so it is not confused with other similar subject.
  * Give pauses of 7 seconds for people to come back to what you were talking.
  * Ask questions to intrigue interest, dont ask obvious or veryhad questions. Mostly open questions ?
* Tools
  * Time and place - brightly lit, feel full
  * Dont just throw a complicated diagram at people, build it along, that way they are not overwhelmed.
  * Not too many words on slides.
  * Reduce distractions on the screen. Title, logo, background, bullets, blah blah
  * Dont lose contact with your audience, use arrows instead of doing a pointer.
