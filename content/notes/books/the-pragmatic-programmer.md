* Kaizen - improving a little everyday to make yourself great
* Being honest and direct and not hiding the problems
    * Never blame or make excuses
    * Think of solutions/options for a problem you faced before talking to stakeholders
* Don’t allow rot to happen, fix even one slight problem with code quality right there
    * Entropy - don’t let it win
    * Broken Window Theory - board it but don’t let it be broken, cause it leads to be more breaking
        * Broken places tend to be broken sooner, and beautiful places tend to become more
* Catalyst of change
    * Start with something like a stone soup and turn it into an awesome soup
    * Trigger curiosity of people by taking the first step
    * Use it to cut through problems of resource limits and permissions
    * Show people a glimpse of the future and they will join you
    * Very important to build a community around your project
* Don’t be a frog in a bowl of water heating up, have the big picture in mind
    * Keep stakeholders involved through the length of the project
* Get software out that is good enough first, perfect it later
* Control yourself from over-engineering
* Knowledge portfolio
    * Program regularly
    * Diversify for long term success
    * Balance between high-risk/high profit skills and low-risk/low profit skills
    * Learn a tech that is not very popular now too (buy low, sell high)
    * Keep reviewing for the above
* Take it a personal challenge to find answers
* Communicate -plan and communicate
    * Know what you want to say - what?
    * Who ? The audience
    * When ? Choose a moment
