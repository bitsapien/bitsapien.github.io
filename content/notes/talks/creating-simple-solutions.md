The talk is originally called "Creating a framework-less, server-less, node-module-less web page"

The goal of the talk was to demonstrate to people the importance of not fitting the solution to the problem, but the vice-versa.

The lack of unnecessary dependencies and opinions in the tools and frameworks you use goes a long way in increasing code maintainability.
