### Things you need

For the rice
* Chicken or any meat
* Spring onions
* Potatoes
* Boiled Rice
* Oyster Sauce
* Soya Sauce
* Fish Sauce
* Oil
* Salt

Egg poach
* Oil
* An egg
* Salt


### Recipe
1. Finely chop the meat, spring onions, potatoes. Remember, they need to be extremely tiny cuts.
2. Add a spoon of oil in a heated pan and add the spring onions, meat together and let it cook for a minute.
3. Add a spoon of soya sauce, let it boil for a bit. Mix it with the mixture you already have in the pan.
4. Add 2 fistfuls of rice and mix everything together
5. Add some oyster and fish sauce to the mixture.
6. Add the chopped potatoes and stir until you think the potatoes have cooked.
7. Make an Egg poach, the way you like it and serve.
