### Things you need
_Serves 4_

For the broth
* Chicken - 250g
* An onion
* Salt

For the spice bag
* A piece of cloth or a paper bag.
* An inch of cinnamon (_dalchini_)
* An onion
* An inch of ginger
* 4 coriander roots or 6-7 seeds (_dhania_)

The noodles
* Rice Noodles (_we used a full big pack of [Top Ramen Curry](https://www.amazon.in/Top-Ramen-Curry-Veg-280g/dp/B0186U375U/)_)

Final Serving
* Chopped spring onions
* Chopped lemon leaves or just some lemon juice


### Recipe
_To make this dish, you need to prepare: the broth, the spice bag, and the noodles, which you could very well do parallely._

The broth
* Heat 2 litres of water enough for the chicken you want to make.
* Add the chicken to this water, and leave it until it comes to a boil, oh! and also add salt.
* The boiling would cause some froth to accumulate on the top, keep removing that using a strainer.
* Add an onion to this, and keep the broth going like an hour and a half.

The spice bag
* Heat up a non-stick pan
* Put the spices listed above onto the pan : ginger, onion, coriander, cinnamon
* Let them burn until their outermost layer starts turning blakc and charred.
* Remove the ones that seem to have reached the corrrect level of "burnt".
* You should now get a nice aroma
* Brush away the charred layer from the spices and pack them in the cloth or a paper bag.
* Drop this into the broth and let the broth boil for another 30 minutes.


The noodles
* Heat up enough water for your noodles (Hint: _The noodles need to be completely submerged_)
* Let them get soft, use a fork to check that.
* Once they are done, transfer the hot water to another container (_We will use this in a later step_)
* Wash the noodles in cold water.
* Finish up with cleaning in hot water that we saved from the earlier step.
* Now spread the noodles to let it dry, the above step helps the noodles to dry quicker and thus not form a cake.


**Bonus** Muoi Ot (Vietnamese Salt)

* Finely slice some red chilli peppers (not the dried ones) and crush them with salt.
* This goes well with Pho, if you want to increase it's spice.

The final serving
* Remove the chicken from the broth and put on a plate
* Shred the chicken a bit
* Now add some noodles in a bowl and add the chicken and the chopped spring onions and lemon leaves to them.
* Add enough broth to this mixture, and finally add _Muoi Ot_ to taste to the _Pho_.
* You are done !
